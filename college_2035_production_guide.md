## 🔧 Assembly & Post-Production Workflow

### Step 1: Generate All 6 Clips

1. Upload each reference image to your chosen AI video tool
2. Use **Image-to-Video (I2V)** mode
3. Paste the corresponding prompt
4. Set camera motion as specified
5. Generate at **1080×1920 (9:16)** resolution
6. Generate 2–3 variations of each clip and pick the best

### Step 2: Assemble in Video Editor

Use **CapCut** (free, mobile-friendly) or **DaVinci Resolve** (free, desktop) for assembly.

**Timeline:**

| Clip | Start | End | Duration | Transition |

| Scene 3 | 0:06 | 0:10 | 4 sec | Cross dissolve (0.3s) |

Add these text overlays in your editor (use a clean sans-serif font like **Inter**, **SF Pro**, or **Montserrat**):

  - [Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/) (free with attribution)
  - [Artlist](https://artlist.io/) (paid, premium)
- Choose a track that builds gradually over 20 seconds

### Step 5: Color Grading

Apply a consistent color grade across all 6 clips:
- **Slight teal-orange split tone** (warm highlights, cool shadows)
- **Lift blacks slightly** for a cinematic look
- **Add subtle film grain** (2-5%)
- **Slight vignette** on indoor shots
- **Boost warmth** on golden hour shots (scenes 4 & 6)

### Step 6: Export Settings

```
Format: MP4 (H.264 or H.265)
Resolution: 1080 × 1920 (9:16 vertical)
Frame Rate: 24 fps (cinematic) or 30 fps (smooth)
Bitrate: 15-20 Mbps (high quality)
Audio: AAC, 320 kbps
Duration: Exactly 20.0 seconds
```
