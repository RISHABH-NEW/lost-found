Core Advantages

Solving the Messy Campus Notice Board Problem

Physical notice boards and unstructured WhatsApp groups make searching hard, compromise student privacy, and cause slow recovery.

🏛️

One Central Database

All lost and found reports from the library, canteen, sports grounds, and academic blocks are collected in one organized database.

🔒

Contact Privacy Guard

Personal phone numbers and contact details are hidden from public scrapers and accessible only to authenticated campus members.

📸

Photo Verification

High quality photo uploads powered by Multer allow rightful owners to clearly identify their missing belongings before in-person handover.

⚡

Instant Keyword Search

Quickly filter by item category, campus zone, or keywords to check if your lost item has already been turned in by someone.

📍

Campus Zone Categorization

Structured location tags such as Computer Lab 2, Central Library, Main Gate, and Canteen eliminate confusion on where to search.

✅

Status Resolution

Once an item is successfully returned, the poster can mark it as Resolved with a single click, keeping the feed clean and up-to-date.

Explore Campus Belongings by Zone

Click on your campus building or common spot to see what has been found or reported lost there.
